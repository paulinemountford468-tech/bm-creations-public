SMARTSHOP

A foldable retail idea by Brian Mountford

A compact, movable shop concept designed to open quickly for trading.

1
BM

The original idea

Brian’s archive note describes wheeled retail sales units with hinged metal doors. Several lockable units could be connected, opened quickly for trading and closed securely afterwards.

WheeledLockableQuick to openQuick to close
2
BM

Two matching units

The later presentation describes two foldable retail units. Each unit is shown as 2 ft × 4 ft × 2 ft when closed.

Dimensions are reproduced from Brian’s later SmartShop presentation sheets.

3
BM

Open display

Opened side by side, the two units are presented as an 8 ft wide display, about 6 ft high and 2 ft deep.

4
BM

Retail settings

The concept sheets describe a flexible unit for different retail settings, including busy high streets, events and temporary market spaces.

High streetMarketsEventsPop-up sales

From idea to working unit

Brian’s autobiography records that he built a SmartShop unit and applied for a patent. The concept combines a movable frame, hinged panels, secure storage and a retail display that can be set up and packed away.
