VIDI LOOK

A CHANCE TO WIN A SHARE IN DOUBLE THE DECLARED JACKPOT PAYOUTS IN EVERY FRIDAY EUROMILLIONS AND SATURDAY NATIONAL LOTTERY DECLARED PRIZE DRAWS

An original concept by Brian Mountford

The weekly routine

Three minutes
once a week

VIDI LOOK is designed to bring people back each week to watch a short video and qualify for that week’s Members Club.

  1. 1

    Register your mobile number

    Open the proposed VIDI LOOK app and register your mobile number.

  2. 2

    Open the featured advert

    Select the advertiser’s logo to open the three-minute video.

  3. 3

    Watch to the end

    Tick the watched box when the video finishes.

  4. 4

    Receive confirmation

    The confirmation would include your weekly membership and the numbers entered for each of the two draws.

VIDI LOOK02

The two weekly draws

Friday and
Saturday

The proposed weekly confirmation gives members two sets of numbers to check against the official results.

Friday draw

EuroMillions

Saturday draw

The National Lottery

If the VIDI LOOK numbers match the jackpot-winning numbers in either draw, the declared jackpot prize payout for that draw would be used to calculate the proposed amount to share.

VIDI LOOK03

The proposed payout

Double the amount.
Share it equally.

If there is a qualifying jackpot match, VIDI LOOK would double the declared payout and divide the total equally among eligible members who make a valid claim.

Example declared jackpot payout£10m
Proposed total after doubling£20m
Example share with 1,000 valid claims£20,000
How a claim would work
Sign in to the proposed VIDI LOOK website and submit your name, full address and registered mobile number for verification.

VIDI LOOK04

For online projects

A reason to
come back

VIDI LOOK could also help online projects attract visitors by featuring their videos in the weekly viewing routine.

Feature a video

An advertiser or project presents its story, product or launch in a short video.

Give viewers a next step

Viewers who want to know more could follow a link to the project’s own website.

Refresh the feature each week

A new video each week could give members a reason to return and give different projects a chance to be seen.
