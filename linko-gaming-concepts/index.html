LINKO MATCH · THE CONCEPT

Nine colours.
Nine segments.
One mystery ball.

Create your own colour ball by filling its nine numbered segments.

Choose any of the nine colours for each segment. You may repeat a colour as often as you wish.

When all nine segments are filled, lock your entry and compare it with the concealed mystery ball.

The challenge

How many colours have you placed in exactly the same numbered positions?

LINKO MATCH · HOW TO PLAY

Choose, fill,
lock and reveal.

  1. Choose a colour. Select one of the nine numbered colours.
  2. Fill a segment. Select a numbered section of the ball. Repeat until all nine are coloured.
  3. Lock your entry. Your colour choices are now fixed for this round.
  4. Reveal the result. Score one match for every colour in the same numbered position as the mystery ball.
  5. Play again. A new game generates a new concealed ball.
