Brian’s account · The invention

A simpler idea for a long flight

Brian recalled seeing a fellow passenger trying to exercise his legs and feet in the limited space on an airline flight. After returning home, Brian designed the PhysioBic Ball: a sponge ball held in a sponge ring, intended as a simple, lightweight and washable exercise aid for the feet, arms and neck.

He described supplying it in a drawstring net bag and sending samples to the NHS and physiotherapist Mr Edward Silk. This page summarizes Brian’s original account in “The Life of Brian.”
